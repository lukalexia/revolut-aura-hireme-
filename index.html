02 · Strategy

A sharper insight than "people want to feel safe."

The audience insight

These are Revolut customers who already have a card. They've heard of single-use protection. They haven't activated it. For Gen Z and younger Millennials, the blocker isn't a knowledge gap. It's a decision paralysis. They know single-use cards work. They've read the specs. The problem isn't information. It's the friction inside their head.

Users don't abandon carts due to security concerns. They freeze because they don't trust unfamiliar merchants with their real card number. This merchant-trust friction creates decision paralysis at checkout.

Source: Payment processing analysis, Adyen & Stripe reports, 2024-2026

At the moment they want to buy, their brain hijacks them. A thousand what-ifs fire at once. They hesitate. They leave. The sale dies before they even touch the card number field.

11 · How we'll know it worked

Strategic signals. Quantitative. Qualitative. Honest.

The KPIs below are the strategic spine. With real data and channel performance, more specific KPIs and benchmarks will be added per channel and per segment.

Top-line metrics
Activation
% of existing Revolut users who activate at least one Aura card during the campaign window. Top-line success metric.
Conversion
% of activators who go on to complete at least one Aura purchase. Measures whether activation translates into real product use, not just one-time curiosity.
Retention lift
Stickiness of Aura activators vs. a matched control cohort of non-activators. Tests whether Aura makes Revolut stickier for the customers we already have. The strategic point of this campaign.
